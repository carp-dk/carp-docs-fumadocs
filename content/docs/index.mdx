CARP is an open-source platform for collecting data from phones, wearables and participants in research and health studies. New to CARP? Read the [introduction](/docs/introduction), then build an app in the [quickstart](/docs/quickstart).

## Flutter plugins and packages

The Flutter plugins and packages developed by the Copenhagen Research Platform:

<Cards>
  <Card title="Health" icon="Heart" href="/docs/flutter-plugins/health">Read and write health data from and to Apple Health and Google Health Connect.</Card>
  <Card title="Mobile Sensing" icon="Sensors" href="/docs/mobile-sensing">Collect data from onboard phone sensors, wearable devices and users.</Card>
  <Card title="CARP Debug Toolkit" icon="Bug" href="/docs/flutter-plugins/debug-toolkit">An in-app debugging toolkit for CARP (and any) Flutter apps.</Card>
  <Card title="Apple Watch Sensing" icon="Watch" href="/docs/sampling-packages/apple-watch">Collect passive sensor data from an Apple Watch in a CARP Mobile Sensing study.</Card>
</Cards>

CARP is an open-source platform for collecting data from phones, wearables and participants in research and health studies. New to CARP? Read the [introduction](/docs/introduction), then build an app in the [quickstart](/docs/quickstart).

## Flutter plugins and packages

The Flutter plugins and packages developed by the Copenhagen Research Platform:

<Cards>
<Card title="Health" href="/docs/flutter-plugins/health" icon="Heart">

Read and write health data from and to Apple Health and Google Health Connect.

</Card>
<Card title="CARP Mobile Sensing" href="/docs/mobile-sensing" icon="Sensors">

Collect data from onboard phone sensors, wearable devices and users.

</Card>
<Card title="Research Package" href="/docs/flutter-plugins/research-package" icon="Assignment">

Surveys, informed consent and structured results for Flutter research apps.

</Card>
<Card title="CARP Debug Toolkit" href="/docs/flutter-plugins/debug-toolkit" icon="Bug">

An in-app debugging toolkit for CARP apps and other Flutter apps.

</Card>
</Cards>

## Sampling packages

Sampling packages add measures to a [CARP Mobile Sensing](/docs/mobile-sensing) study:

<Cards>
<Card title="Context Sampling Package" href="/docs/sampling-packages/context" icon="Location">

Collect location, activity, geofence, mobility, weather and air quality data.

</Card>
<Card title="Survey Sampling Package" href="/docs/sampling-packages/survey" icon="Quiz">

Run surveys, cognitive tests and informed consent as app tasks.

</Card>
<Card title="Health Sampling Package" href="/docs/sampling-packages/health" icon="MonitorHeart">

Collect health data from Google Health Connect and Apple Health.

</Card>
<Card title="Audio Sampling Package" href="/docs/sampling-packages/audio" icon="Mic">

Collect noise levels and audio recordings from the phone's microphone.

</Card>
<Card title="Connectivity Sampling Package" href="/docs/sampling-packages/connectivity" icon="Wifi">

Collect network status, Wi-Fi, nearby Bluetooth devices and iBeacons.

</Card>
<Card title="Communication Sampling Package" href="/docs/sampling-packages/communication" icon="Call">

Collect phone calls, text messages and calendar events.

</Card>
<Card title="Apps Sampling Package" href="/docs/sampling-packages/apps" icon="Apps">

Collect the installed apps and per-app usage time from Android phones.

</Card>
<Card title="Apple Watch Sensing" href="/docs/sampling-packages/apple-watch" icon="Watch">

Collect passive sensor data from an Apple Watch in a CARP Mobile Sensing study.

</Card>
<Card title="Polar Sampling Package" href="/docs/sampling-packages/polar" icon="EcgHeart">

Collect heart rate, ECG, optical heart rate and motion from Polar sensors.

</Card>
<Card title="Movesense Sampling Package" href="/docs/sampling-packages/movesense" icon="VitalSigns">

Collect heart rate, ECG, movement and tap events from Movesense sensors.

</Card>
<Card title="Movisens Sampling Package" href="/docs/sampling-packages/movisens" icon="FitnessTracker">

Collect activity, heart rate, EDA and skin temperature from Movisens sensors.

</Card>
<Card title="eSense Sampling Package" href="/docs/sampling-packages/esense" icon="Headphones">

Collect button presses and motion data from an eSense earable.

</Card>
</Cards>

---
title: Capabilities and permissions
description: The capabilities, background modes and privacy strings that the watch target needs.
---

A sensor on watchOS needs three things before it produces data:

1. a **capability** on the target;
2. a **background mode**, if it runs while the wrist is lowered;
3. a **privacy string** that explains why it is used.

Set everything on this page on the **`CarpWatch Watch App`** target.

## Capabilities

Select the watch target → **Signing & Capabilities** → **+ Capability** and add:

| Capability | Needed for |
|---|---|
| **Background Modes** → Audio | The `microphone` background session, and any sound sensing |
| **Background Modes** → Workout processing | The `workout` background session |
| **Background Modes** → Location updates | Location sampling |
| **HealthKit** (tick **Background Delivery**) | Heart rate |

This writes `CarpWatch Watch App.entitlements`:

```xml title="CarpWatch Watch App.entitlements"
<?xml version="1.0" encoding="UTF-8"?>
<!DOCTYPE plist PUBLIC "-//Apple//DTD PLIST 1.0//EN" "http://www.apple.com/DTDs/PropertyList-1.0.dtd">
<plist version="1.0">
<dict>
	<key>com.apple.developer.healthkit</key>
	<true/>
	<key>com.apple.developer.healthkit.background-delivery</key>
	<true/>
</dict>
</plist>
```

<Callout type="info">

You do **not** need HealthKit, microphone or location capabilities on the `Runner` (iOS) target for this package, unless your phone app also needs them.

</Callout>

## Background modes

Adding the capabilities above writes the background modes for you. Verify that the watch target ends up with:

```xml
<key>WKBackgroundModes</key>
<array>
	<string>workout-processing</string>
</array>
<key>UIBackgroundModes</key>
<array>
	<string>audio</string>
	<string>location</string>
</array>
```

<Callout type="warning" title="Match the background session type">

The background mode has to match the `backgroundSessionType` you set on the `AppleWatchDevice`: `microphone` needs the `audio` mode, and `workout` needs `workout-processing`. Without the matching mode, the session fails to start and the watch app is suspended as soon as the participant lowers their wrist.

</Callout>

## Privacy strings

watchOS refuses to show a permission prompt whose purpose string is missing. Xcode 15 and later generate the watch app's `Info.plist` from build settings, so the easiest place to add these keys is the target's **Info** tab.

| Key | Needed for | Example value |
|---|---|---|
| `NSHealthShareUsageDescription` | Heart rate | "This study reads your heart rate from the Health app for X." |
| `NSHealthUpdateUsageDescription` | Required alongside the above | "This study does not write any data to the Health app." |
| `NSMicrophoneUsageDescription` | Sound sensing and the `microphone` background session | "The microphone is used to measure the noise level around you. No audio is recorded or saved." |
| `NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription` | Location | "Your location is collected to describe where you spend your time." |
| `NSLocationAlwaysAndWhenInUseUsageDescription` | Location in the background | Same as above |
| `NSBluetoothAlwaysUsageDescription` | Bluetooth scanning | "Nearby Bluetooth devices are scanned to estimate social context. No connection is made." |

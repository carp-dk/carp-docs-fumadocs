`NOISE` (`dk.cachet.carp.noise`) samples the sound level from the phone's microphone in short windows and adds one [`Noise`](https://pub.dev/documentation/carp_audio_package/latest/media/Noise-class.html) measurement per window, with four statistics in decibels. It is an [event-based](/docs/mobile-sensing/measure-types#event-based-and-one-time-measures) measure on the phone, on Android and iOS.

The measure stores no sound: the probe reduces the microphone samples to levels as they arrive. The constant is on [`MediaSamplingPackage`](https://pub.dev/documentation/carp_audio_package/latest/media/MediaSamplingPackage-class.html), and the levels come from the `noise_meter` plugin.

## Add the measure to a protocol

`NOISE` schedules its own sampling windows, so start it once with an `ImmediateTrigger()`. This task samples for 5 seconds every 30 seconds instead of the default:

```dart
protocol.addTaskControl(
  ImmediateTrigger(),
  BackgroundTask(
    measures: [
      Measure(
        type: MediaSamplingPackage.NOISE,
        samplingConfiguration: PeriodicSamplingConfiguration( // [!code highlight]
          interval: const Duration(seconds: 30), // [!code highlight]
          duration: const Duration(seconds: 5), // [!code highlight]
        ), // [!code highlight]
      ),
    ],
  ),
  phone,
);
```

A shorter interval gives more measurements, but the microphone is on more often and the battery drains faster.

## Configuration

`NOISE` uses a [`PeriodicSamplingConfiguration`](https://pub.dev/documentation/carp_mobile_sensing/latest/domain/PeriodicSamplingConfiguration-class.html):

| Property | Type | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `interval` | `Duration` | 5 minutes | Time from the start of one window to the start of the next. |
| `duration` | `Duration` | 10 seconds | Length of each window. |

Both properties are required when you create the configuration. The defaults in the table come from the package's default sampling scheme, which samples for 10 seconds every 5 minutes.

## Sampling windows

While the task runs, the probe works in a cycle:

1. One interval after the task starts, and then once per interval, it opens a window. With the defaults, the first window opens 5 minutes after the task starts.
2. During the window, it listens to the microphone and turns each buffer of samples into a reading with two levels. On Android, a buffer holds 6,400 samples, about 145 milliseconds of sound at 44.1 kHz.
3. When the window closes, it computes the four statistics from the readings and adds one measurement. The measurement's `sensorStartTime` and `sensorEndTime` are the start and end of the window.

If the task stops during a window, the readings of that window are discarded. A window also adds no measurement when fewer than two of its readings have a finite level, because the standard deviation is then undefined. A window of digital silence has no finite readings, and the probe reports an error for it instead.

## Decibel values

The microphone samples are scaled to values between -1 and 1. The two levels of a reading come from the lowest and the highest sample in its buffer:

```text
mean level = 20 × log10(32768 × (|lowest sample| + |highest sample|) / 2)
peak level = 20 × log10(32768 × highest sample)
```

The values are decibels relative to the smallest step of a 16-bit sample, so full scale is about 90.3 dB. They are not calibrated sound pressure levels: the same sound gives different values on different phone models. Compare noise levels within the same phone, or calibrate each phone model against a sound level meter.

## Platform differences

<Tab value="Android">

- `audio_streamer` records 16-bit mono samples at 44.1 kHz with Android's `AudioRecord`.
- CARP Mobile Sensing (CAMS) starts the measure only when the participant has granted `RECORD_AUDIO`.

</Tab>
<Tab value="iOS">

- `audio_streamer` taps the microphone through `AVAudioEngine`, with 44.1 kHz as the preferred sample rate. iOS may use another rate.
- CAMS starts the measure without checking the microphone permission.

</Tab>

## Limitations

- **Not calibrated:** the levels depend on the phone's microphone and differ between phone models.
- **Background sampling on Android:** Android 11 or later lets a background app use the microphone only from a foreground service of type `microphone`. The CAMS setup declares its foreground service as `dataSync|specialUse`, not `microphone`, so test background sampling on the phones of your study.
- **Background sampling on iOS:** sampling continues in the background only with the `audio` background mode.
- **Delayed start:** the first window starts one interval after the task starts, and a window in progress is discarded when the task stops.

## Data

Each `Noise` holds the statistics of one window, computed from the readings with a finite level:

| Field | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `meanDecibel` | `double` | Mean of the readings' mean levels, in dB. |
| `stdDecibel` | `double` | Sample standard deviation of the readings' mean levels, in dB. |
| `minDecibel` | `double` | Lowest mean level of a reading, in dB. |
| `maxDecibel` | `double` | Highest peak level of a reading, in dB. |

For the measurement envelope around the data, see [Measurements and data](/docs/mobile-sensing/domain-model#measurements-and-data). An example of the data:

```json
{
  "__type": "dk.cachet.carp.noise",
  "meanDecibel": 47.83251874509615,
  "stdDecibel": 3.9171604375130887,
  "minDecibel": 41.06923398265123,
  "maxDecibel": 68.72041385620347
}
```
